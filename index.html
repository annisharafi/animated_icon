Flowers that never say goodbye

We freeze flowers mid-bloom mid-bloom so time never gets to finish the job.

  • We catch the lotus before dusk closes it

    Suspended just before it folds inward for the night, so it stays open, always.

  • We hold the dandelion before the wind can ask

    Every seed kept in place, mid-flight, mid-wish, nothing let go before you're ready.

  • We stop the rose at its fullest breath

    The exact moment before the first petal falls kept there, on purpose, forever.
